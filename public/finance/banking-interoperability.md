For a beginner, the appeal is simple: send money like sending a message, anywhere, fast and cheap, but the risk is that more connection means more shared failure points and shared data, so the calm view weighs the convenience against the dependency and the privacy, because interoperability is a rail that helps everyone, including the ones you didn't intend. The appeal is flow; the risk is linkage.
