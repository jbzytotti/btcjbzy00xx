For a beginner, the appeal is real yield from assets otherwise idle, but the risk is that providing liquidity is not lending — you own the pool's exposure and its flaws, so the calm miner sizes tiny, uses established pools, and reads the impermanent-loss math before supplying, because the fee is earned against a position that can move against you, and the yield is the price of that risk, not a gift. The appeal is income; the risk is the position.
