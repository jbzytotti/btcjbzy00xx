## How to Flip Calmly: A 10-Step Guide

### 1. Price buy

### 2. Full cost

### 3. Pad reno

### 4. Hard stop

### 5. Know exit

Set the sale price before buying; the number leads. The exit known. Price. Real. Calm.

### 6. Smart fix

### 7. Watch rate

### 8. Speed sell

List ready and price to move; empty weeks bill you. The sell speeds. Move. Real. Calm.

### 9. Permit early

### 10. Stay calm
