## How to Read Candles Calmly: A 10-Step Guide

Reading calmly is context and confirm. These ten steps help beginners.

### 1. Read trend

Know the bigger trend before you trust any shape. The trend reads. Big. Real. Base.

### 2. Name shape

Learn the hammer, star, and engulf with clear rules. The shape names. Rules. Real. Calm.

### 3. Check volume

Rising volume backs a real turn; low volume warns. The volume checks. Back. Real. Caution.

### 4. Wait confirm

Demand the next candle agrees before you act. The confirm waits. Next. Real. Careful.

### 5. Avoid lone

A pattern with no context is a coin flip in costume. The lone avoids. Context. No. Calm.

### 6. Place matters

The same shape means different things in different spots. The place matters. Spot. Real. Careful.

### 7. Paper test

Try the read on paper before you size the real trade. The test paper. Size. Real. Calm.

### 8. Small size

Only afford-to-lose sums when you test a pattern. The size small. Loseable. Real. Safe.

### 9. No algo bait

Patterns get run to shake buyers; don't bite the bait. The bait avoids. Shake. No. Caution.

### 10. Stay calm

The candle is a hint; the confirm is the proof. The calm holds. Proof. Balanced. Survive.
