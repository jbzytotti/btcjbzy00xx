What to weigh:

- **Draw line** — connect rising lows (or falling highs).
- **Pullback trade** — buy the touch, not the top.
- **Confirm bounce** — small bounce proves the line holds.
- **Stop under** — a broken line ends the trend; stop there.
- **Broken risk** — a break means the ride is over.
- **Late entry** — chasing the top pays the chaser's loss.
- **Scale in** — buy pieces at the touch, not all.
- **Plain draw** — simple line beats clever angles.
- **Crowd memory** — the line is respected, not a law.
- **Calm** — ride the middle, not the extreme.**

## How to Swing With Trendlines Calmly: A 10-Step Guide

Swinging a line is a calm habit. These ten steps help beginners.

### 1. Draw line

Connect two or more lows (or highs) cleanly. The line draws. Clean. Real. Base.

### 2. Wait touch

Trade the pullback to the line, not the top. The touch waits. Pull. Real. Calm.

### 3. Confirm bounce

A small bounce proves the line still holds. The bounce confirms. Hold. Real. Careful.

### 4. Scale in

Buy pieces at the touch, not all at once. The scale buys. Pieces. Real. Careful.

### 5. Stop under

Place stop below the line; break means exit. The stop sets. Below. Real. Caution.

### 6. Avoid top

Don't chase the high after the line ran. The top avoids. High. Real. Calm.

### 7. Plain draw

Simple line beats clever angled guesses. The draw stays. Simple. Real. Calm.

### 8. Small size

Only afford-to-lose sums on the swing. The size small. Loseable. Real. Safe.

### 9. Exit break

When the line breaks, leave; don't hope. The break exits. Leave. Real. Careful.

### 10. Stay calm

Ride the middle; the line is a guide. The calm holds. Middle. Balanced. Survive.

## Mistakes With Swing Trendlines

Chasing the top after the line ran and buying the fall.

Holding past a broken line, hoping the trend returns.

Drawing clever angles instead of a plain respected line.
