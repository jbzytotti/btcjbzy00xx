- **Depth view** — see bids and asks stacked at every price.
- **Support clue** — thick bid can mean a floor under price.
- **Resistance clue** — thick ask can mean a ceiling above.
- **Spoof risk** — fake size appears then is pulled fast.
- **Iceberg risk** — big hidden orders behind small ones.
- **Trend read** — watch the shift, not one snapshot.
- **Confirm price** — depth is a clue, match it to movement.
- **Noise** — ignore the lone whale that vanishes.
- **Calm** — a fast book can lie; read the pattern.
- **Plan** — use depth as a tool, not a crystal ball.**
